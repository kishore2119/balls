A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Good questions.
Simple answers.

You bring the idea.
Orbit brings the bounce.

Which animations can I use right now?+

Ring Escape is ready to use. The other cards are concepts for future animations, and are clearly marked “Coming later.” We’ll add them one by one.

Can I use my own music and sounds?+

Yes. In the editor, upload a bounce sound or a background track, choose a built-in melody, or import MIDI notes. Bounce sounds and melody notes follow the animation’s collision timing.

What can I export?+

Create vertical 9:16 videos at 720p or 1080p, with 30 or 60 frames per second. Choose MP4 or WebM, with audio or without. The editor also saves still images.

Will my settings be saved?+

Your current setup and saved presets stay in this browser, along with uploaded sounds. You can also download your settings as a project file from the editor.
