Personal data & privacy

This notice explains personal information handled by Orbit Studio and how to request access, correction, or deletion.

Operator: Orbit Studio · Updated: 23 October 2026 · gamer.robot.in@gmail.com

Information handledYour rightsRequests & complaints

What personal information is handled?

The current app has no sign-in, user profiles, customer account database, payment processing, advertising, or analytics scripts. We do not currently collect account names, account email addresses, passwords, or billing information through an account system.

InformationPurpose and handling
Emails you send us, your reply address, and personal details in the messageUsed to answer support questions, handle privacy requests, verify requests where needed, and resolve complaints. Handled in the operator’s mailbox and by the email providers delivering your message.
Connection details such as IP address and requested URLThe serving host receives ordinary connection details to deliver website files. Hosting logs require their own retention and security arrangements.

Privacy correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable legal recordkeeping requirements. We may ask for limited information to verify a request. Do not send passwords, payment details, or identity documents in a routine request.

Projects and files in the editor

Animation projects, presets, and imported files are saved in this browser so you can continue editing. The editor does not send them to an Orbit Studio server. Project names, photos, or recordings can contain personal information; use material you have permission to use. Ordinary project backup and editing controls are in the editor.

Request personal data deletion

You can also request withdrawal of consent for consent-based personal-data processing, raise a privacy complaint, or make a nomination request under the applicable DPDP process. A nominee can exercise rights in the event of death or incapacity.

Give the operator an opportunity to resolve a DPDP complaint before approaching the Data Protection Board of India through the applicable official process. See the government’s DPDP page for official information.

Legal timetable

The Act and Rules have phased commencement. Core processing obligations and data-principal rights are scheduled for eighteen months after the November 2025 notifications, in May 2027. This page does not certify the service as fully compliant. Account-data workflows will need to be implemented when a user account backend is added.
